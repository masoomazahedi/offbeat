Which fields kept growing through a summer with no rain?

Around Herat it does not rain from June to October, so land that grows a crop in those months is getting water from somewhere. NASA and ISRO's NISAR radar sees through cloud and dust and passed over the valley ten times. OFFBEAT reads those ten passes hectare by hectare and marks the patches whose season ran out of step with the fields beside them.

Data

NISAR L-band GCOV, path 70 frame 19, provisional Level-2 products from NASA's Alaska Satellite Facility. NISAR is a joint NASA and ISRO mission. Radar power is averaged from 10 m to 100 m cells, and the scene-wide brightness shift on each date is removed.

Method

A hectare changed when its HV echo at least doubled between its weakest and strongest pass. A summer peak is strongest on 18 August or later; an early peak is strongest in June or July. A hectare is in step when its ten values rise and fall with the average of at least four changed hectares within about half a kilometre. The comparison follows the reasoning of Bazzi et al. (2019), who used it with Sentinel-1 in Catalonia.

About

The two photographs on this page, in the sidebar and behind the heading, are AI-generated illustrations, watermarked as such. They are decoration: they are not satellite imagery, not pictures of Herat, and no result on this page comes from them.

OFFBEAT is a prototype for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge "Dancing with the SARs". Built with the assistance of Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. No individual farms or people are identified; the smallest unit shown is one hectare.
